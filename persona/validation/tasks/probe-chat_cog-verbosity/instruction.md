# Messaging chat

You're texting back and forth with a friend who's catching up with you. They
just messaged you:

> **hey! what have you been up to lately?**

In your **first message**, reply the way you'd text a friend. Then keep the
conversation going for at least one more exchange, reacting naturally to
whatever they say back.

Write your messages however feels natural to you — there are no length or style
requirements.
