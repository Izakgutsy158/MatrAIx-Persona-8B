# Explain-it chat

You're answering a short everyday-tech question in a chat. An assistant collects
your answer and can respond to it.

The question: when you type a website's address into your browser and hit Enter,
what actually happens between your computer and that website so the page ends up
on your screen?

## What you can do in this product

- Send chat messages. Write your explanation, send it, and react to the
  assistant's reply.
