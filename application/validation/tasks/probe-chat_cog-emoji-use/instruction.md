# Post an app review

You're using a chat-style review assistant to help you post a review of an app
you love.

In your **first message**, write the full one-paragraph review itself: pick an
app you use all the time and genuinely love, say what it is and why it makes your
day better. Write it exactly the way you would actually post it publicly.

Then continue for at least one more exchange, reacting to the assistant's reply
(for example, refining or confirming your review).

Write your messages however feels natural to you — there are no style requirements.
