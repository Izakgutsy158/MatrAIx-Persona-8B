# Explain-it chat

You're chatting with an assistant that collects short answers to everyday tech
questions.

In your **first message**, answer this question in your own words:

> When you type a website's address into your browser and hit Enter, what
> actually happens between your computer and that website so the page ends up on
> your screen?

Write out your full answer in that first message.

Then continue for at least one more exchange, reacting to the assistant's reply
(for example, adding to or clarifying your explanation).

Write your messages however feels natural to you — there are no length or style
requirements.
