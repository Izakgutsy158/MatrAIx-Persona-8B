# Intro chat

You're using a chat app that collects a short spoken-style intro from you.

Send **one message** answering this question: *what's the main thing you do —
your work or a hobby you care about — and why does it matter to you?* Write it
the way you naturally would; this is just you sharing your answer, not a
back-and-forth.

After you send it, the app acknowledges receipt and you're done.
